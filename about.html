Peptide research / Lead AI contributor

Multimodal AI for peptide research

As lead AI contributor, I worked on preparing peptide data and applying multimodal learning to MHC class I and II research. The pipeline combines peptide sequences, HLA information, and biological features to explore cancer-associated peptide classification.

The research code includes data merging and filtering, duplicate and label-conflict checks, and removal of label-leaking features. Modeling experiments use BLOSUM62 sequence encoding, ESM-2 protein embeddings, and three-stage PyTorch architectures.

Interacting agents · Collective prediction
AI research / In development

ACC: a dynamical approach to learning

I developed ACC as a system of interacting agents whose internal states evolve over simulated time. Recurrent connections and input connections shape that evolution; the final collective state becomes a prediction.

Learning combines prediction-error feedback with a Hebbian-style association rule to adapt connections. This work explores how useful predictions can emerge from a changing population of agents.

Dynamical systemsRecurrent connectionsHebbian learning

Preparing this research for submission to the Journal of Artificial Intelligence Research.
